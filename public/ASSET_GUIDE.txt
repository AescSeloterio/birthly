BIRTHDAY WEBSITE ASSET GUIDE

Recommended folders:

/memories/       photos used for memories and albums
/albums/         album cover images
/letters/        optional envelope/letter artwork
/videos/         birthday video messages (.mp4)
/background/     optional full-screen background image

Examples:
/memories/first-date.jpg
/albums/date-nights.jpg
/videos/message-from-me.mp4
/background/ours.jpg

Then update the matching paths in /lib/data.ts.
